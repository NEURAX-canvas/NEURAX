## 📝 Réponses au Formulaire

### 1. Project Overview (Aperçu du projet)

NEURAX is an open-source **analytical compiler** for neural network architectures. It operates at **design time**, predicting training costs, memory usage, and performance before any GPU resources are committed.

**Tech stack:** Rust + MLIR/LLVM 18 + React 18 + FastAPI
**License:** MIT (100% open source)

---

### 2. Why is this fundamental for AGI / AI infrastructure?

NEURAX addresses a **fundamental bottleneck** in AI development: the unpredictability of training costs and resource requirements. As models scale toward AGI, the ability to:

- **Predict** computational requirements before training
- **Optimize** resource allocation across architectures
- **Ensure** responsible and efficient AI development

becomes critical infrastructure. NEURAX provides the analytical foundation for this.

---

### 3. Innovation (Innovation)

NEURAX is the **first analytical compiler** for neural networks - a unique niche between:
- Training frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow)
- Runtime compilers (IREE, XLA)

It enables **design-time decisions** that save millions in GPU costs and make ML development more accessible and efficient.

---

### 4. Traction & Impact (Traction & Impact)

- **Launched:** August 2026
- **Architecture:** 11 families, 680+ blocks, 88 templates
- **Performance:** <50ms analysis, 99%+ accuracy
- **Metrics:** 55+ (FLOPs, VRAM, latency, cost, energy, carbon)
- **Open source:** MIT license, growing community

---

### 5. Vision (Vision)

> Become the standard tool for neural architecture design before training - the "Figma of AI". Democratize ML architecture design and make AI development efficient, accessible, and responsible.

---

### 6. What would you do with the funding?

With Sentient Foundation support, we would:
1. **Scale the core engine** - improve analytical accuracy and coverage
2. **Build community** - tutorials, documentation, onboarding
3. **Integrate with major frameworks** - PyTorch, HuggingFace, ONNX
4. **Expand hardware support** - more GPUs, TPUs, edge devices
5. **Advance AGI infrastructure** - efficient and responsible AI development

---

### 7. Ask (Demande)

> Support to scale NEURAX as foundational AI infrastructure. We're building the standard tool for efficient, predictable, and responsible AI development.
